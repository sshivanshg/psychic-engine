Infosys Q4 FY26 Earnings Conference Call — Edited Transcript (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE, for testing only)

Revenue and Growth
Revenue for the quarter was USD 5.04 billion, growing 6.6% year on year in constant currency.
Growth was led by Financial Services and Energy, Utilities & Resources, while Communications
remained soft. We signed large deals with total contract value of USD 4.5 billion, of which 56%
were net new.

Margins
Operating margin for the quarter expanded to 21.1%, up roughly 90 basis points year on year,
driven by our Project Maximus efficiency program, lower subcontractor costs, and pricing
discipline. Net margin was 18.2%. We continue to target an operating margin band of 20% to 22%.

Guidance
For FY27 we are guiding revenue growth of 2% to 4% in constant currency, within the 20% to 22%
margin band. Demand in financial services is recovering; discretionary spend in retail remains
cautious.

Attrition and Headcount
Voluntary attrition on a trailing twelve-month basis declined to 13.4%, down from 17.8% a year
ago. Total headcount was approximately 320,000, and utilisation excluding trainees was 85.7%.

Capital Allocation
The Board recommended a final dividend of INR 22 per share. Under our capital allocation policy
we expect to return about 85% of free cash flow to shareholders over the policy period via
dividends and buybacks.

Analyst Q&A
Q: How should we think about the margin trajectory from here?
A: We see room for further margin improvement from automation and AI-led delivery, but we will
reinvest part of the gains into talent and sales capacity. We are comfortable defending the 20 to
22 percent band.

Q: How is generative AI affecting the deal pipeline?
A: Generative AI is now embedded in a majority of our large deals. It is modestly deflationary on
legacy revenue but expansionary on new transformation programs; the net effect on the pipeline is
positive.
